Additional practice areas

Technical support across MHPSS and protection

Moses’s work includes community-based and community-led mental health and psychosocial support, protection, suicide prevention and response research, and the bidirectional integration of MHPSS across humanitarian and development sectors.

His technical and programmatic work spans research and assessments; programme design and evaluation; strategic and technical advisory work, including on-site practice support; and capacity strengthening through training, coaching and mentoring. This includes support for culturally grounded, evidence-informed and locally led approaches that centre affected communities’ knowledge, agency and leadership.

Additional CV-documented capabilities

  • Regional technical leadership and cross-country coordination.
  • MHPSS and public health; humanitarian protection, inclusion and safeguarding.
  • Programme strategy, design and quality assurance, including peacebuilding and conflict-sensitive programming.
  • Partnership and stakeholder engagement; staff coaching, mentoring and capacity strengthening.
  • Resource prospecting, proposal development and budget support.
  • Evidence generation, research and learning products.
  • Refugee mental health, coping, resilience and psychosocial wellbeing.
  • Integration of MHPSS across humanitarian, development and peacebuilding programmes.
